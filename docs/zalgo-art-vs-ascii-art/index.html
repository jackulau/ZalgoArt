Zalgo art and ASCII art

Both draw pictures with text. ASCII art arranges characters in rows and columns. Zalgo art is one line of characters, each carrying a stack of marks. They hold together in different places.

ASCII art needs a fixed-width font

ASCII art is a grid. The picture is made of rows of characters, and it only reads as a picture when every character is the same width, so that each one sits exactly under the one above. A fixed-width font guarantees that.

In a proportional font the characters have different widths. A space is narrower than an underscore, a slash is narrower than a bracket, and the rows slide out of line.

Zalgo art is one line

Zalgo art does not depend on rows lining up, because there is only one row. Each column of the picture is one letter with a stack of combining marks above and below it. The stack belongs to its letter and travels with it, so there is nothing to slide out of line.

What zalgo art needs instead

Zalgo art swaps one dependency for another. It does not need a fixed-width font, but it does need three things.

  • A font that stacks marks. Some fonts draw every mark in the same spot and the picture comes out flat. Others stack only so many: Safari stops at 30.
  • Room above and below. The stacks are taller than their line, and many sites cut off what leaves the post. Blank lines have to be reserved to hold them.
  • A length that fits. Every mark is a character, and every platform has a limit.

For the platform you pick, Zalgo Art checks the length and the room, and warns where it knows the marks will not stack. See character limits by platform for the limits it uses.

What each can show

ASCII art can use any character for its shape or its darkness, and it can be as many rows tall as you like. Zalgo art has a few shades, set by how heavy the marks are, and its height is the height of a stack.

Which to use

Use ASCII art where a fixed-width font is certain: a terminal, a code block, a plain text file. Use zalgo art where the text will be shown in a proportional font, and check the result on the devices you care about.

To try it, turn an image into text or draw one. For the background, read what zalgo text is.
