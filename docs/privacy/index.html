Privacy

Zalgo Art runs in your browser. This page says what the site does with what you give it, as the site stands on the date shown.

What stays on your device

A picture you choose or drop on the page is read by the page itself and turned into text there. The site does not upload it. The words you type and what you draw are handled the same way.

The site's own code makes no requests after a page has loaded. Each page loads one thing from another site: Google's advertising script, described under Advertising below. There are no fonts, images or analytics from other sites.

What is stored in your browser

One thing: the drawing on the Draw canvas, so that it is still there when you come back. It is kept in your browser's local storage for this site under the name zalgoart.pad.1. It is not sent anywhere. Pressing Clear empties it, and clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.

The site's own code sets no cookies. Google's advertising script may set cookies of its own, as described below.

The clipboard

When you press Copy text, the page puts the text on your clipboard. If you paste a picture into the page, the page takes that picture from the paste and converts it the same way as a file you chose. It does not read the clipboard at any other time.

Advertising

The site is set up to show advertising from Google AdSense. Every page loads Google's advertising script from pagead2.googlesyndication.com. That script belongs to Google, not to this project. It runs in the page and makes requests of its own to Google and its advertising partners.

Google and its partners use cookies and similar identifiers to show and measure ads, and may choose ads based on your earlier visits to this site and to others. How Google uses information from sites that use its services explains what is collected. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings, and opt out of other companies' personalised ads at aboutads.info.

The site does not hand your picture, your words or your drawing to the advertising script. What that script does once it is on the page is governed by Google's policies, not by this one.

Accounts and analytics

There are no accounts, and the site has no analytics of its own.

What the hosts see

The site is served by Vercel, and the older address by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, they receive the request your browser makes for a page: your IP address, the address of the page, and what your browser says about itself. They keep ordinary logs of those requests under their own privacy policies.

The tool's settings can be written into a page's address, for example ?mode=draw. Anything in an address you open is part of that request.

The command line tool

The command line tool runs on your computer and makes no network requests.

Questions

The source is public, so every statement here can be checked against it. To ask about any of it, open an issue on GitHub.
