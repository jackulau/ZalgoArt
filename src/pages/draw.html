How to draw zalgo art

  1. Draw on the canvas. The preview updates as you go.
  2. Choose where you will post it, in the menu at the top. With Auto-fit on, the drawing is sized to that platform's limits.
  3. Press Copy text and paste it into your post.

The grid

The canvas is ruled with the grid the text uses. Each column of cells becomes one letter, and each cell holds one mark. The dashed line is the baseline, the row of letters the marks hang on: what you draw above it grows up from the letters, and what you draw below grows down.

Tools and inks

Brush
Freehand strokes, from {{ui:brush.min}} to {{ui:brush.max}} in size.
Line
A straight line, as thick as the brush.
Box and Oval
Filled shapes, dragged from corner to corner.
Fill
Floods an area with the chosen ink.
Dark, Mid, Light, Erase
Three shades of ink and an eraser.
Undo, Redo, Clear
Step back and forward through your strokes, or empty the canvas.

Your drawing is kept

The drawing is saved in this browser, so it is still there when you come back. The site does not send it anywhere. Clear empties it. The privacy page says where it is stored.
