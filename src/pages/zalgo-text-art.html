How to write in big letters

  1. Type in the Words box.
  2. Choose where you will post it, in the menu at the top.
  3. Leave Auto-fit on for the largest letters that fit, or set Letter size and Rows yourself.
  4. Press Copy text and paste it into your post.

How the letters are built

The letters come from a small pixel font built into the tool. Each column of a letter is one character with a pile of marks, dark marks where the letter has ink and light marks filling the holes inside it, so that every column ends level with its neighbours.

This is different from glitch text, which piles random marks on letters of ordinary size. Here the marks are the letters.

Sizes and rows

There are {{ui:tsize.max}} letter sizes. With Auto-fit on, the tool takes the largest size that passes the platform's limits, on one row if the words fit and on two if they do not. The same words come out larger where the limit is longer.

One row of letters either stands on the line or hangs from it, whichever needs fewer marks. With two rows, the first stands on the line and the second hangs below it.

What it can write

Letters, digits and common punctuation. Capital and small letters are drawn the same, and an accented letter is drawn as its plain letter. A character the built-in font has no shape for is left out, and the tool names what it left out.

Adding a caption

The Caption box puts a line of ordinary text above the letters, for a post that reads normally and then shows the lettering under it.
